# CaseStudyChallenges

CLAUDE.md Part 21's Challenges section: `{issue, resolution}[]` pairs
documenting what went wrong and how it was resolved.

## Why this section exists at all

"Most agencies hide problems. We document them" (CLAUDE.md Part 21) — an
honest account of friction resolved is more convincing than a story where
nothing ever went wrong.
