# AiPhilosophySection

CLAUDE.md Milestone 13 §§13-14's AI Philosophy and Byld Mascot, combined
into one section — the spec introduces Byld here as "the public
manifestation of this philosophy," so splitting the philosophy from its
embodiment into two sections would divide one idea in half.

## "Meet Byld" reuses the real AI Companion

The mascot introduction embeds the homepage's `AiCompanionHighlight`
directly rather than building a second, bespoke "meet the mascot"
interaction. It's already a real conversation preview pulled live from
the actual response engine (not scripted demo copy) plus a real entry
point into the same AI Companion this page's Hero and Team section also
link to — reimplementing that here would either duplicate it or risk
drifting from what the AI Companion actually says.

## `data/ai-assisted-areas.ts`

Each of the seven areas states what AI accelerates _and_ what stays a
human decision — "AI is an accelerator, not a substitute" only means
something when it's concrete per area, not a slogan repeated once at the
top.
