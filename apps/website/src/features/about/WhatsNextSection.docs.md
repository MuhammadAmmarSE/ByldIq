# WhatsNextSection

CLAUDE.md Milestone 13 §§15-18: Growth/Evolution, Future Vision, Labs, and
Careers, consolidated into one section. All four carry the same
instruction — never fabricate — so four separate honest-empty blocks
would mostly repeat "nothing to show here yet" in different words.

## Growth/Evolution folded into Future Vision

No verified milestones exist anywhere in this codebase (real dates,
clients, revenue, employee counts) to present as a chronological company
history, so this section doesn't invent one. `data/future-platform.ts`
reuses CLAUDE.md Part 1's own stated platform evolution verbatim — real
direction, honestly labeled as direction rather than achievement (some
stages already exist, like AI Companion and Knowledge Center; most
don't yet).

## Labs and Careers

Both state their current honest status plainly instead of a placeholder
grid of invented experiments or job listings. Careers routes interest
through the real AI Companion (`useAiCompanion().open`) rather than a
fabricated application form or contact address that doesn't exist yet.

you're interested" opens the AI Companion.
