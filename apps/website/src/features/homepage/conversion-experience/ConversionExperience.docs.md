# ConversionExperience

CLAUDE.md Part 19's Final CTA / Conversion Experience — a decision engine
with five next steps (`DecisionCards`), one highlighted per journey but
never forced, plus a calendar preview, FAQ, and newsletter signup.

## Decision routing

Each card routes differently: **Book Discovery** reveals an illustrative
`CalendarPreview` in place; **Use BuildPath** and **Explore Knowledge**
navigate to their real pages (Phases 9 and 10); **Talk to Byld** opens the
AI Companion (Phase 8) directly; **Download Resources** shows a toast
explaining the resources aren't published yet, rather than a dead link or
broken download.

## Honesty

The calendar and newsletter are real, working UI — clicking a slot or
submitting an email genuinely updates state and gives feedback — but
neither has a backend yet. Both say so in their own copy ("we'll follow
up by email to confirm", "no discounts, no spam") rather than pretending
to book or send anything.
