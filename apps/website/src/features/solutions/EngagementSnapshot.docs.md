# EngagementSnapshot

Milestone 10's "Timeline / Team composition / Estimated investment" —
three quick engagement facts per solution.

## Distinct from DeliveryFramework and SuccessMetrics

`DeliveryFramework` walks through the shared nine-stage engineering
_process_ (Discovery → Optimization) — the same for every solution, not
a duration. `SuccessMetrics` describes outcomes. Neither answers "how
long, with whom, and roughly how much" — the three fields this section
renders (`deliveryTimeline`, `teamComposition`, `investmentGuidance`).

## Investment guidance is deliberately not a dollar figure

CLAUDE.md Part 7 rules out inventing numbers, and the AI Companion's own
`RESPONSES.pricing` already states the company's real position: "We
don't quote a price without understanding the product first... BuildPath
will give you a realistic investment range." A fabricated per-solution
dollar figure here would contradict that stated philosophy, not just be
unverifiable — so `investmentGuidance` is a qualitative sentence that
ends by pointing to BuildPath for an actual range, on every solution.

## Team composition reuses real role names

Roles reuse CLAUDE.md Part 17's Suggested Team list (Product Manager,
UX Designer, Frontend/Backend/AI Engineer, QA, DevOps) rather than
inventing new titles per solution.
